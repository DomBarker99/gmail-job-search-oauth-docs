Usage terms

Effective October 8, 2026. Gmail Job Search is a private personal-use tool, not a public service or application-submission service.

Permitted use

Only the operator's own Gmail accounts may be connected. Account access must be knowingly authorized by the owner and permitted by any relevant Workspace administrator. Google authorization remains optional and may be revoked.

The connector requests Gmail read-only access for local archiving, refresh, and job-search correspondence review. It must not send email, modify Gmail messages or labels, delete live mail, submit or withdraw employer applications, upload resumes, or accept employer terms.

Owner control

Synchronization is manually invoked unless the owner separately authorizes scheduling. The owner controls local retention, archive location, and any optional selected-content AI review. Authorization to read Gmail does not authorize unrelated external communications or employer actions.

Limits

This experimental personal tool is provided without a guarantee of availability, archive completeness, or accurate application-status inference. The owner should verify important correspondence and portal requirements against their original sources and keep appropriate independent backups.

Use and transfer of Google data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements, and the privacy policy.
