Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026. This policy describes Gmail Job Search, a private local desktop application operated only for its owner's own accounts. Local archiving and job-search refresh functionality are being configured. This website serves public application information only.

Google data accessed

The application requests only Gmail read-only authorization. Depending on the locally invoked operation, it can access account email address and mailbox counts, messages and their headers, bodies and attachments, labels, thread and message identifiers, timestamps, and change-history metadata. The scope covers the whole authorized mailbox. Account provenance is preserved separately.

Purpose and use

Google data is used for the owner's local offline mailbox copies, incremental synchronization, and authorized job-search correspondence review. Complete mailbox storage is separate from relevant-message processing. Data is not used for advertising, sold, publicly published, or used by this application to train general-purpose AI models.

OAuth setup and account verification use only the metadata needed to confirm the authorized account and read-only access. They do not fetch message bodies or attachments.

Storage and protection

OAuth client configuration is stored in an owner-only local application-configuration directory outside the public website, source repository, dashboard, and iCloud-synced Desktop/Documents. Account refresh tokens are stored in the local macOS Keychain, not in project files, command-line arguments, or public logs.

Offline mailbox copies remain on the owner's computer and are not served by this website. Mailbox archive encryption and permanent archive placement are separate owner-controlled decisions. This policy does not claim that archive files are already encrypted. The public documentation hosting service receives only these informational pages, never mailbox data or OAuth credentials.

Sharing and optional AI-assisted review

The local connector communicates with Google to authorize access and retrieve authorized data. It does not send complete mailboxes to an AI provider or to the public documentation host.

The owner may separately authorize AI-assisted review of selected job-search-relevant information. Only the selected relevant material may be provided to the owner's chosen assistant/provider for that requested function, subject to Google's Limited Use requirements and appropriate provider settings. No such mail-content transfer is enabled by the OAuth setup alone. Unrelated mail, credentials, and account tokens must not be included.

Retention, deletion, and control

Locally archived messages are retained until the owner chooses to remove them. A message's deletion in live Gmail need not erase its offline archive copy. Disconnecting an account or revoking access stops future authorized retrieval but does not automatically delete existing local archives. Token revocation and local credential removal are owner-controlled operations.

The owner can review or revoke this app's access through Google Account connections. The application does not send messages, change Gmail message/label state, submit employer applications, or accept employer terms.

Limited Use and policy updates

Gmail Job Search's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This policy must be updated before a change to data handling that is not described here. The app has no public registration or third-party customer accounts.
